# Every page here carries <meta name="robots" content="noindex,nofollow">, which is the
# reliable way to keep these staging copies out of search results. Blocking with
# Disallow would prevent crawlers from ever reading that noindex tag -- and would risk
# blocking Medium's own importer, which is the only reason this host exists.
